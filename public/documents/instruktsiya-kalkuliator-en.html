1

Signing in to the portal

Open the partner portal's sign-in page and enter your login. The sign-in screen itself is in Ukrainian — the calculator's own interface is fully switchable to English, see step 3.

  1. Email — your own address, the one you gave us when your access was set up.
  2. Password (Пароль) — this one we give you when setting up your access.
  3. Click the button below (Увійти — "Sign in").

Forgot your password? There's a link right under the form ("Забули пароль?" — Forgot password).

2

Your dashboard — finding the calculator

After signing in you land on your dashboard. In the top menu, click Commercial Offer — that's the calculator for building quotes.

Good to know: the same dashboard has a link to the full PDF product catalogue (an English version too — "EN catalogue") and to the "Довідник по лінійках IN WOOD" reference guide on colours, frame/trim/casing and terminology (Ukrainian only), if anything in the calculator itself is unclear.
3

How the calculator is laid out

Before filling anything in, a quick look at the screen:

  1. Language & currency bar — already set to English for your account; also lets you convert prices to EUR/USD (covered in step 8).
  2. "Client and tariff" — the client's name and phone/email for this quote.
  3. "Add item" — choose the model, colour, size and all the add-ons for one line of the quote here.
  4. Table on the right — every item you add lands here, along with the running total.

The calculator opens with EN already selected for your account — field names, buttons and the table are all in English from the start. The UA button next to it switches everything to Ukrainian, if you ever need to.

4

Client details

Enter the client's name and phone (or email) for this quote.

"Consultant phone" and "Comment" are optional — fill them in only if you need to.

5

Door model, colour and size

In the "Add item" block, pick the door line (ETALON, NOMINAL, FREZZATTI, PERFETTO, etc.) — it's already set to the first one in the list. Then, in order:

  1. Model — the specific door code (e.g. ET-01).
  2. Colour — from the films available for this model.
  3. Width and height — from the standard size range.
If extra fields appear — "Edge colour", "Insert colour" or "Opening side" — they're required specifically for this model. Fill them in too, otherwise the "Add item" button further down will stay disabled.
6

Add-ons, cutout, quantity

Further down are the frame, front trim, back trim and door casing — picked the same way as colour, from a dropdown. All four fields are fully independent of each other: you can pick only the front trim without the back one, only a frame without a casing, or none of them at all — whatever this particular order doesn't need, just leave as "not selected". Then:

  1. Cutout — no cutout / lock cutout / full hardware cutout.
  2. Soundproofing — tick if needed (ETALON line).
  3. Quantity — how many of this door the client needs.

Right under the form you'll see a photo of the selected door and a price breakdown for every component — check it against what the client asked for before adding the item.

One thing to watch for: if you pick a "concealed mount" frame (STANDART or LUX) for ETALON, the trim and casing fields disappear from the screen. That's not a bug — they simply don't apply to that type of frame.
7

Adding the item and the totals table

Once everything required is filled in, the "Add item" button becomes active. Click it: the line moves into the table on the right, and you can immediately start adding the next item (a different model or colour) the same way.

  1. Each item shows a full price breakdown by component.
  2. "Remove" — delete an item if you made a mistake.
  3. Discount — in percent or in the account's currency, if agreed with the client.
  4. The running total for the whole quote.
  5. Three save buttons — covered in the next step.
8

Currency conversion and saving the file

Next to the language switch is the currency choice: UAH / EUR / USD. Pick EUR or USD and enter the exchange rate in the field on the right — every price in the table and the total recalculate instantly.

Once the quote is ready, under the total there are three buttons:

  1. Save PDF — downloads the finished file right away, the easiest way to send it to the client.
  2. Save file — an alternative way to save the document.
  3. Print — opens the print dialog if you need a hard copy immediately.

That's it — the commercial offer is ready.

9

Separately: frame, trim or casing without a panel

If the client only needs a frame, trim or door casing without the door itself — don't look for it among the door models. In the top "Line" select, choose "Linear items (frame/trim/casing separately)" — a shorter, separate form appears:

  1. Line — which collection the item is from (ETALON, NOMINAL, etc.).
  2. Type — frame, trim or casing.
  3. Item — the specific product from the list.

It's added to the table the same way, with the "Add item" button — price and quantity work exactly like a regular item.

10

Separately: hardware (handles, hinges, locks, etc.)

Same "Line" select — choose the hardware brand you need ("Hardware MVM", "Hardware Anselmi + AGB", etc.). Then:

  1. Category — handles, hinges, locks, stops, mechanisms, etc.
  2. Article — the specific item; its photo and material appear right under the select.

Click "Add item" the same way — done.

Hardware item names and materials currently display in Ukrainian regardless of the language switch — the article code itself (e.g. A-1209 SN/CP) is language-neutral and fine for ordering.

11

In short: required fields

If you haven't filled in a colour, size, opening side or any other field required for that particular model, the "Add item" button simply won't become active. Go through the form from top to bottom and check that everything is filled in.

Questions about colours, frame/trim/casing or terminology — see "Довідник по лінійках IN WOOD" in your dashboard. If anything in the calculator doesn't work as expected — just get in touch with us.
